Reports diagnostics from the Ameba linter for Crystal code: style, correctness, and performance findings exactly as the configured ameba binary reports them (JSON output over the current buffer).

Enable Ameba and configure its executable under Settings | Languages & Frameworks | Crystal | Ameba Linter. Disabling this inspection also stops live Ameba highlighting in the editor.

Example: items.map(&.name).compact reports Performance/CompactAfterMap [Ameba] with an explicit ameba --fix action.
